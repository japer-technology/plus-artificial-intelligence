### interfaceCopy.plainBody — pronoun binds to the result, not AI
- **Location:** `interfaceCopy.plainBody`, second sentence
- **Current:** `…企喺使用佢嘅決定背後。`
- **English:** `…stands behind the decision to use it.`
- **Issue:** English "it" refers to using **AI**; the Cantonese "佢" is ambiguous and most naturally binds to "個結果" (the result).
- **Suggested:** `…企喺使用 AI 嘅決定背後。`
- **Severity:** LOW
- **Round 1:** carried forward (verified still present)

### Section 20 — title uses the adjective "neutral"
- **Location:** `sections[20].title`
- **Current:** `中立`
- **English:** `Neutrality`
- **Issue:** "中立" is the adjective "neutral"; a section heading should be nominal ("中立性"), matching the other headings in the file.
- **Suggested:** `中立性`
- **Severity:** LOW
- **Round 1:** carried forward (verified still present)

### Section 7 — "substantially generated" rendered "mainly generated" (new in round 2)
- **Location:** `sections[7].body`, "MAY publish content substantially generated by AI"
- **Current:** `責任方可以發布主要由人工智能生成嘅內容。`

- **Issue:** "主要由" means "mainly", drifting toward the "predominantly" concept section 22 renders separately ("主要由人類原創"). "Substantially" here means "to a significant extent".

- **Round 1:** new in round 2
