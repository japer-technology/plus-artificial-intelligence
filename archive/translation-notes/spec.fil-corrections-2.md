### Section 19 — "convention" rendered as "agreement"
- **Location:** `sections[19].body`, closing paragraph
- **Current:** `…gumanap bilang pangkalahatang kasunduan.`

- **Severity:** LOW
- **Round 1:** carried forward (verified still present)

### Section 7 — "substantially generated" weakened to "a large part" (new in round 2)
- **Location:** `sections[7].body`, "MAY publish content substantially generated by AI"
- **Current:** `…maglathala ng nilalamang malaking bahagi ay ginawa ng AI.`
- **English:** `The responsible party MAY publish content substantially generated by AI.`
- **Issue:** "malaking bahagi" means "a large part", which drifts toward "predominantly" — the concept section 22 deliberately renders as "nangingibabaw". "Substantially" here means "to a significant extent".

- **Severity:** LOW
- **Round 1:** new in round 2
