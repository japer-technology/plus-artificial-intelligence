# Welcome to +AI

## Meet +AI

### A simple mark for AI-assisted work—and for the people who stand behind it

We’re excited to share a compact notation for work materially assisted by
artificial intelligence.

When a person or organisation signs their name with `+AI`, they make two things
clear:

- artificial intelligence helped create the work; and
- the named person or organisation accepts responsibility for releasing it.

AI can help us think, write, design, analyse and build. It cannot take
responsibility. `+AI` keeps assistance visible and accountability human.

The idea is deliberately small: two characters that make an important
distinction. As AI becomes part of everyday work, clear attribution gives us a
shared way to be transparent without surrendering authorship or responsibility.

May your intelligence be ever responsible,

— Eric Mourant +AI
