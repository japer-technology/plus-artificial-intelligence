The +AI Declaration

AI is already in the room.

It helps us write, think, design, code, research and create.

The important question is not whether AI was used. It is who takes responsibility for the result.

AI can generate the work. It cannot stand behind it.

A human can.

That is what +AI means:

AI helped. I take responsibility.

When I place +AI after my name, I am saying AI materially assisted the work. I chose what to accept, reject and release, and I take responsibility for that choice.

+AI is not an apology, warning label or certificate. It is a declaration of ownership.

A signature says: I stand behind this.

+AI adds: AI helped me get here.

Use AI openly. Use it creatively. But when you publish the result, put your name on it and take responsibility.

No permission. No registration. No certification. No gatekeeper.

Use AI openly.
Keep responsibility human.

AI helped. I take responsibility.

+AI
