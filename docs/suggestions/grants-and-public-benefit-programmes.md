# Grants and Public-Benefit Programmes

## Suggestion

Seek grants for translation quality, accessibility, AI literacy, provenance,
open tooling, research, archiving, and support for schools, libraries, media,
nonprofits, civil society, and under-resourced communities.

## Before implementation

Publish grant terms and deliverables, screen funders for mission conflicts, keep
outputs open where safe, and consider stipends, clinics, and fee waivers.
