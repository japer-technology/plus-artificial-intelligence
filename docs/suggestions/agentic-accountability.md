# Agentic Accountability

## Suggestion

Explore declarations for delegated AI-agent actions that identify the delegating
human or organisation, bounded scope and expiry, approval to publish or deploy,
action receipts, revocation, escalation contacts, and contribution chains.

## Before implementation

Keep this outside the simple v0.1 meaning until delegation, multi-party
responsibility, authority, failure, and human-release semantics are understandable.
