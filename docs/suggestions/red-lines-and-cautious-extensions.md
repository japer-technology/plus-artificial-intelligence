# Red Lines and Cautious Extensions

## Suggestion

Maintain an explicit watchlist for social feeds, rankings, automated AI-use
inference, hosted mail, vendor sponsorship, identity integrations, blockchain
anchoring, reputation scores, and other high-risk extensions.

## Before implementation

Reject payment for use, mandatory registration or identity, forced tool or prompt
disclosure, paid placement, personal-data sales, covert tracking, biometric proof,
automated publication in another's name, and any transfer of responsibility to AI.
