# Brainstorm — Programme 09: Funding and Sustainability

> Companion to [`09-funding-and-sustainability.md`](../09-funding-and-sustainability.md). Every member suggestion is decomposed here into **definitive features** and **pre-implementation decisions**; each has one agent request in [`requests/`](requests/). Workflow: open a request, record your choices in its §1, then give the file to your agent.

## Programme-level decisions (affect more than one request)

1. **Core boundary as the shared invariant.** Every request's §3 opens with the catalogue's core boundary verbatim: payment buys convenience, scale, support, or managed operations — never legitimacy, rank, or permission. It is the free-floor invariant (IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN §4.1) and the load-bearing rule of the whole programme; no member may soften it.
2. **Influence-creep launch-review rule.** The recurring attack on the catalogue is "payment buys X". Every request's §3/§4 restates the free-floor covenant's per-item definitions (no directory rank, no evidence labels, no decision rights, no data access, no changed outcome) as concrete must-nots — not just intentions — and each self-check walks those per-item definitions, per the programme risk "Influence creep" (IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN §11 and the programme file's Risks).
3. **Sequencing gate before money flows.** No paid or hosted surface here opens before the Programme 1 governance/financial-transparency rules and the Programme 8 static-first failure-mode statement exist for it (programme Sequencing item 4). Each hosted/paid request ships its failure-mode statement, threat model, privacy analysis, and cost model in its `docs/services/<slug>.md` — the hosted-service gate discipline (IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN §11.6), surfaced in each request's §2/§5 and the spec's own §5.2 items.
4. **§11 cross-cutting placement.**
   - **Free-quota sizes for the read API** (set with cost-discipline modelling; Programme 8 owns the model, Programme 4 owns the surface) → `api-scale-and-reliability` D1, naming both owners in the decision text.
   - **Sponsorship watchlist assessment** (published before the first sponsor is accepted) → `sponsorship-policy` D1, naming `red-lines-and-cautious-extensions` (Programme 10) as the gate owner.
   - **"Meaningful adoption" metric** gates P3/P4 extensions — `membership-model` and `managed-hosting-and-domains` depend on it; each carries a §2 note stating the dependency, with the metric itself owned by Programme 10 / Programme 4 (not defined here).
5. **Multi-language standard folding.** The draft standard ([`best-practice-multi-language.md`](../../../analysis/best-practice-multi-language.md), rules R1–R16, tiers T0–T4, status vocabulary, resolution order) governs every localised surface here; public pages and materials are T2 (translated with per-document fallback, English governs). Five primary requests carry it explicitly per the assigned placement: `support-and-donations` (public page T2), `training-consulting-and-pilots` (materials T2), `publications-and-merchandise` (canonical wording T0 verbatim; storefront T2), `sponsorship-policy` (public ledger page T2), `membership-model` (join page T2). Judgement placements: `api-scale-and-reliability` (pricing/status surfaces T2; API/schema wire keys T0 never translated), `managed-hosting-and-domains` (pricing/status/manage-UI chrome R1/R4/R12/R13; customer content T4), `privacy-respecting-analytics` (event catalogue + opt-in control copy T2), `grants-and-public-benefit-programmes` (programme pages T2 — beneficiaries are multilingual; deliverable content T4), `priority-review-services` (queue-rules + pricing T2; findings follow their track, T4).
6. **Output locations.** Static public pages live as `site/<name>.html` beside the existing ~90 theme pages; hosted-service designs ship as `docs/services/<slug>.md` with their Programme 8 gates (mirroring Programme 07); structured machine-readable records ship as `site/*.json` (sponsorship ledger, analytics event catalogue); the SOW template lives under `docs/templates/`. Paid services are optional layers *around* the commons and never add files the commons depends on (IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN §3).
7. **Screening-rubric harmonisation.** `sponsorship-policy` (mission-alignment screening) and `grants-and-public-benefit-programmes` (mission-conflict screen) both delegate final rubric wording to `governance-and-stewardship` + `red-lines-and-cautious-extensions`; their D-rubric decisions name the same owner and the same hard-decline list so the project does not end up with two divergent screens.

Order-line/Sequencing tension: `publications-and-merchandise`'s mini-plan "Order" line reads "P3/P4 — THE-PLAN already demands posters/the book; the storefront follows the free kit", while the Sequencing list places it in the P4 group. Following the Order line (per the request conventions), its Phase is recorded as P3/P4; the tension is flagged in its member block below.

## Member-by-member brainstorm

### support-and-donations — Support and Donations
- **Request:** [`requests/support-and-donations.md`](requests/support-and-donations.md)
- **Definitive features:** one static, versioned, mirrorable `site/support.html`; free-floor list (notation, canonical meaning, reviewed translations, no-account builder, portable formats, basic checking, governance record, correction/revocation) and the paid-services list (each linked); the no-legitimacy/rank/permission statement; visible donation-vs-purchase distinction routed per the financial-transparency payment-recipient table; dated/versioned funding priorities linking the latest report + archive; one-time + recurring options (explicit consent, stated cadence/amount, cancellable anytime); refund policy (donations discretionary, purchases per commercial terms); honest tax-status statement; supporter-anonymity option linking the materiality threshold; non-financial-help route to the contribution ladder; the no-influence/no-evidence-status prohibition.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Payment processor (third-party vs direct); D2 — Refund window and donation refund posture.
- **Open questions / risks:** processor choice and purchase refund window are the two spec §10 / digest "Open:" items (D1–D2; the refund number is pending legal review). Risk: the page drifting into implying a donation buys something — guarded by the visible distinction and the on-page prohibition.

### sponsorship-policy — Sponsorship Policy
- **Request:** [`requests/sponsorship-policy.md`](requests/sponsorship-policy.md)
- **Definitive features:** sponsorship programme policy + assessment gate (watchlist-adjacent, preconditioned on the published assessment owned by red-lines-and-cautious-extensions); mission-alignment screening rubric (with the seven prohibited effects as automatic declines); the prohibition list (no specification control, vendor preference, directory rank, user-data access, category exclusivity, evidence advantage, finding suppression); factual, dated recognition placed outside normative content and evidence surfaces; public sponsorship ledger (no amount ordering, carries the no-influence statement); lifecycle (proposed/assessed/accepted/ended/declined, ended keeps its dated credit, corrections as dated errata); financial-transparency disclosure as funded deliverables.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Watchlist assessment gate (pre-first-sponsor); D2 — Mission-alignment screening rubric; D3 — Logo recognition.
- **Open questions / risks:** the assessment is owned by red-lines-and-cautious-extensions (Programme 10) and must precede the first sponsor (D1); rubric wording (D2) and logo size/placement (D3) are the spec §10 / digest "Open:" items, the latter pending mark-policy review. Risk: recognition creeping into evidence surfaces — guarded by the "never in normative content or evidence surfaces" must-not.

### membership-model — Membership Model
- **Request:** [`requests/membership-model.md`](requests/membership-model.md)
- **Definitive features:** optional individual + organisation membership funding a future custodian and working groups; join page rendering the free-floor statement and non-member-rights enumeration before any payment; published, bounded, convenience-only tiers/benefits; low-cost tier + sponsored-seat/fee-waiver route (deferring eligibility to grants); advisory ballot rules (one vote per member, no contribution weighting, never touching specification or free floor); community seats reserved in member-funded working groups; conflict/recusal controls; free, immediate, no-penalty cancellation; membership as a disclosed income category; hosted membership system with its Programme 8 gates.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Tiers, prices, and benefit sets; D2 — Before vs after custodian.
- **Open questions / risks:** tiers/prices need cost-discipline input (D1); before-vs-after custodian is left open by spec §10 but the mini-plan Order line already resolves it to "after the custodian path is real" (D2). The "meaningful adoption" metric (Programme 10/Programme 4) gates this P3 extension — noted in §2, not defined here. Risk: membership becoming a gate or rank — guarded by the non-member-rights enumeration and the advisory-only ballot rule.

### grants-and-public-benefit-programmes — Grants and Public-Benefit Programmes
- **Request:** [`requests/grants-and-public-benefit-programmes.md`](requests/grants-and-public-benefit-programmes.md)
- **Definitive features:** grant acceptance process (published terms, deliverable, funder, amount band, completion status); published mission-conflict screen (funder declines with recorded reason); open-licence, file://-safe, mirrorable deliverable publishing (default open, closure only for safety/privacy/legal); stipends for named open work (never certification); free, bounded clinics with no obligation or upgrade path; fee waivers routed through the membership sponsored-seat route on public-benefit grounds; beneficiary classes served without membership, data disclosure, or ceded rights; a completed open deliverable never later closed or paywalled.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Mission-conflict rubric; D2 — Clinic scheduling (per sector vs per region).
- **Open questions / risks:** rubric wording (D1) and clinic capacity scheduling (D2) are the spec §10 / digest "Open:" items. The rubric is harmonised with sponsorship-policy's screening rubric (programme-level decision 7). Risk: funder capture — guarded by the no-control/no-data/no-rank must-nots and open-licence outputs.

### api-scale-and-reliability — API Scale and Reliability
- **Request:** [`requests/api-scale-and-reliability.md`](requests/api-scale-and-reliability.md)
- **Definitive features:** paid tier over the public read surface (high-volume quotas, SLAs, dedicated support, webhooks, synchronisation) with public data never the product; documented free quota covering community use; versioned API/schema (additive within a major, breaking only in a new major + migration note); enumerated quotas/rate limits/fair-use limits linked to the cost model; long-lived immutable cache headers over snapshots; honest paid-tier SLAs with published measurement and best-effort free tier; opt-in, revocable, event-scoped webhooks/sync; deprecation with fixed notice + graceful degradation; abuse controls (threat model, rate limits, no-account incident reporting); privacy analysis (no fingerprinting/cross-site profiling/inferred tool use); status page/changelog/RSS incident communication; Programme 8 gates.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Free-quota size (joint owners: Programme 8 cost-discipline model, Programme 4 public-snapshots-and-api surface); D2 — Regional delivery timing.
- **Open questions / risks:** the numeric free quota is fixed against the cost model once published (D1); regional delivery is first-launch vs later per its cost basis (D2). Risk: the free path becoming theoretical — guarded by the "quota covers community use, never so small as to make the free path theoretical" rule.

### managed-hosting-and-domains — Managed Hosting and Domains
- **Request:** [`requests/managed-hosting-and-domains.md`](requests/managed-hosting-and-domains.md)
- **Definitive features:** optional managed hosting of profiles/registers/custom domains/private deployments; cost-based pricing with the no-legitimacy statement; guaranteed portability (free export in portable formats, no justification); explicit customer ownership (operator claims/licences none, no training/ads use); time-limited renewal with a documented grace period, lapse degrading to a labelled read-only/tombstoned state with declarations intact at canonical identifiers; suspension by published policy with appeal, never deleting records; shutdown with advance notice and final export; published data location (jurisdictions/processors); non-biometric recovery (codes/control proofs); per-instance threat model; self-hosted fallback from the offline pack; measured status pages; versioned long-term archival packages; custom domains as address convenience, never proof of identity.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Grace-period length and lapse schedule; D2 — Private deployment timing.
- **Open questions / risks:** grace-period length (D1) and private-deployment timing (D2) are the spec §10 / digest "Open:" items. The "meaningful adoption" metric (Programme 10/Programme 4) gates this P4 extension — noted in §2. Risk: the operator acquiring rights in customer records, or recovery demanding identity documents — guarded by the ownership and non-biometric-recovery must-nots.

### privacy-respecting-analytics — Privacy-Respecting Analytics
- **Request:** [`requests/privacy-respecting-analytics.md`](requests/privacy-respecting-analytics.md)
- **Definitive features:** versioned event catalogue (purpose/fields/retention/aggregate-only per event) published before any collection; absolute rejections (ad-tech, fingerprinting, tracking cookies/pixels, cross-site/device profiles, inferred tool/AI use, contact lists, invasive reader tracking); opt-in default-off with immediate/complete opt-out and a plain-language shared control component; measurement preferring server-side aggregate counts then user-initiated events; per-event bounded retention with scheduled deletion/irreversible aggregation; published aggregation thresholds with "fewer than N" low-count suppression; no-sale and no-cross-service-profile rules; analytics never on the dependency path; failure-mode for any hosted analytics surface.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Default retention periods per event family.
- **Open questions / risks:** retention periods per event family are the sole spec §10 / digest "Open:" item (D1), set jointly with privacy-and-data-minimisation (Programme 8). The low-count minimum defers to research-observatory's disclosure threshold (k = 10 by default) and collection timing follows Programme 4 dashboards — both §2 notes, not decisions here. Risk: measurement becoming surveillance — guarded by the absolute rejections and catalogue-before-collection.

### training-consulting-and-pilots — Training, Consulting, and Pilots
- **Request:** [`requests/training-consulting-and-pilots.md`](requests/training-consulting-and-pilots.md)
- **Definitive features:** services page (keynotes, workshops, consulting, governance facilitation, policy mapping, accessibility, integration, research design, pilots) with no-certification/no-legitimacy statements; statement-of-work template (client, deliverables, exclusions, conflicts, languages, named end point); advice strictly separated from certification; consultants never holding/implying specification authority; reusable learning published openly under the contribution terms and accessibility floor; mandatory handover materials (anti founder-time dependency); conflict disclosure + recusal per governance; paid pilots inheriting pilots-and-case-studies design/consent rules; client-data privacy/threat model; revenue reported through financial-transparency with no influence over registry/moderation/disputes.
- **Decisions:** D1 — Consultant roster; D2 — SOW redaction degree.
- **Open questions / risks:** whether a named-consultant roster is maintained without becoming an endorsement list (D1), and the degree of client-confidential redaction in published SOW summaries (D2), are the spec §10 / digest "Open:" items. Risk: consulting reading as certification — guarded by the no-certification statements and the no-authority rule.

### priority-review-services — Priority Review Services
- **Request:** [`requests/priority-review-services.md`](requests/priority-review-services.md)

Risk: review-quality capture — guarded by the identical-standards rule and the no-outcome-change must-nots (programme risk "Review-quality capture").
