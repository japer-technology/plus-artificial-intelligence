# Brainstorm — Programme 10: Research, Standards, and Deferred Scope

> Companion to [`10-research-standards-deferred.md`](../10-research-standards-deferred.md). Every member suggestion is decomposed here into **definitive features** and **pre-implementation decisions**; each has one agent request in [`requests/`](requests/). Workflow: open a request, record your choices in its §1, then give the file to your agent.

## Programme-level decisions (affect more than one request)

1. **The red-lines doc is the single gate for the deferred register.** `red-lines-and-cautious-extensions` is the one authority that `independent-assurance-and-certification`, `marketplace-and-matching`, and `agentic-accountability` defer to before any exploration. Those three produce decision/policy documents plus precondition records only — never the deferred service — and each cites its RL IDs (§5.2 item 12 of the red-lines spec) and records "preconditions not yet met" as its deliverable. This is the control for the **deferred-register drift** headline risk.
2. **The "meaningful adoption" metric is defined once, referenced everywhere.** It lives in `public-adoption-dashboard` (Programme 4, the data owner, per IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN §11) and is *referenced*, never redefined, as the gate in `independent-assurance-and-certification`, `marketplace-and-matching`, and `standards-engagement`. Each of those requests carries a decision (D1) that adopts the dashboard metric verbatim rather than inventing a local threshold.
3. **Forbidden-zone enforcement for standards engagement.** The normative meaning itself, the `+AI` mark identity, and the red lines are never proposed to an external body; every engagement runs only under a governance-approved mandate naming its documents and forbidden zone, so a single eager partner cannot capture the mark's meaning. Enforced in `standards-engagement` (D3 + §3), which is the **premature-standards-engagement** headline risk.
4. **Watchlist-assessment ownership.** `red-lines-and-cautious-extensions` owns the assessment template and publishes the **blockchain-anchoring** assessment (the one watchlist item with no owning spec). The sibling-owned watchlist items — hosted mail relay (`privacy-preserving-email-relay`, Programme 6) and identity integrations/SSO-SCIM (`organisation-workspaces`, Programme 7) — are recorded in the red-lines doc as *deferrals with pointers* to their owning spec's assessment, not re-assessed here.
5. **Multi-language standard placement.** Five requests carry the project standard: `regulatory-phrasing-packs` and `pilots-and-case-studies` (primary: published example texts/outcomes are T1/T2, English governs, reviewer metadata per pack), plus `provenance-interoperability` (mapping documents — field names are T0, explanatory prose T2), `standards-engagement` (published mandates are T1/T2), and `experiments-and-metrics` (published artifacts and multilingual-equivalence studies). The four pure policy/decision docs (`red-lines-and-cautious-extensions`, `independent-assurance-and-certification`, `marketplace-and-matching`, `agentic-accountability`) are English-only internal governance records with no localised surface and do not carry the §6 line.
6. **Phasing reconciliation.** The mini-plan `Order` lines govern the `Phase` field in each request. `red-lines-and-cautious-extensions` is rendered `P1/P2` per its Order line (the programme header's `Phase:` line and the `Sequencing` list both say `P2` — flagged as a tension below). `marketplace-and-matching` and `agentic-accountability` say `deferred` in their Order lines; this is mapped to the template's `P4-deferred` vocabulary, matching the programme header's "re-opening the deferred register only via its gates" in P4.

## Member-by-member brainstorm

### red-lines-and-cautious-extensions — Red Lines and Cautious Extensions
- **Request:** [`requests/red-lines-and-cautious-extensions.md`](requests/red-lines-and-cautious-extensions.md)
- **Definitive features:** a versioned policy document (`docs/red-lines-and-cautious-extensions.md`) as the single source of truth; nine red lines RL-1…RL-9 with stable identifiers and canonical wording (spec §5.2 items 1–9); the watchlist (social feeds, rankings, automated AI-use inference, hosted mail, vendor sponsorship, identity integrations, blockchain anchoring, reputation scores) with per-item status; the assessment template (proposal, risk, safeguards, sunset, approving body) and recording process (§5.2 item 11); the sibling-citation rule (cite RL IDs, state how each stays clear — §5.2 item 12); the governed amendment rule (§5.2 item 13); a machine-readable red-line/watchlist/assessment record (`docs/red-lines-and-cautious-extensions.json`, §5.3); the published blockchain-anchoring watchlist assessment (cross-cutting, before any anchoring ships).
- **Decisions:** D1 — Red-line review interval; D2 — Watchlist expansion mechanism; D3 — Blockchain-anchoring assessment posture; D4 — Sibling-owned watchlist items (hosted mail, identity/SSO).
- **Open questions / risks:** Phase tension — the mini-plan Order line says `P1/P2` while the programme header `Phase:` line and the `Sequencing` list both say `P2` (Order line followed here). Digest "Open:" (`review interval; watchlist expansion`) and spec §10 (review interval; watchlist expansion incl. voice-cloning / autonomous-deployment candidates) are both decided (D1–D2). Risk: **deferred-register drift** — this doc is the single gate; without it, the three deferred members get built anyway.

### pilots-and-case-studies — Pilots and Case Studies
- **Request:** [`requests/pilots-and-case-studies.md`](requests/pilots-and-case-studies.md)
- **Definitive features:** a pilot-plan template (scope, participants, measures, duration, end point, support, conflicts, languages — §5.2 item 1); a consent + publication-approval process (explicit recorded consent, withdrawal honoured, participant approval of final identifying text — §5.2 items 2, 4); a close-out template (outcomes vs measures, failures, reusable outputs — §5.2 item 9); the failures-published-with-equal-care rule (§5.2 item 5); reusable templates/checklists published openly and meeting the accessibility floor (§5.2 item 7); a named end point per pilot (§5.2 items 1, 9); no rankings/certification framing (RL-9 — §5.2 item 10); child-safe-education precondition for school pilots (§5.2 item 8); static plan/close-out pages linked from changelog/RSS (§5.4).
- **Decisions:** D1 — Observatory feed (anonymised learnings + thresholds); D2 — Localisation posture (English-first T2, reviewed translations for the pilot's languages).
- **Open questions / risks:** digest "Open:" (`feed learnings into observatory`) and spec §10 (whether close-outs feed anonymised learnings into the research observatory, and thresholds) are both decided (D1). Risk: **pilot publication ethics** — publishing failures is the point but participant privacy is absolute; the close-out approval step is the control (never skipped).

### provenance-interoperability — Provenance Interoperability
- **Request:** [`requests/provenance-interoperability.md`](requests/provenance-interoperability.md)
- **Definitive features:** a versioned field-mapping document per external format — C2PA, IPTC, Schema.org, and signature formats — listing every mapped field, direction, and semantic note (§5.2 items 1–6); `no equivalent` recording for unaligned IPTC cases (§5.2 item 4); the C2PA `check-failed` reporting rule (external validation kept separate from declaration labels — §5.2 item 3); open, offline-capable reference implementations for the C2PA/IPTC/Schema.org mappings (§5.2 item 7); the checker-integration contract requiring external provenance as a distinct labelled section (§5.2 item 8 — the checker code itself is Programme 3's); versioned releases with changelog + deprecation notes (§5.2 item 9).
- **Decisions:** D1 — Schema.org property choice; D2 — Machine-readable mapping registry.
- **Open questions / risks:** digest "Open:" (`Schema.org property choice; machine-readable mapping registry`) and spec §10 (which Schema.org properties; whether to publish a machine-readable mapping registry) are both decided (D1–D2). Risk: re-validating or re-certifying external records — guarded by the honesty invariants (no claiming C2PA validation the project did not perform).

### regulatory-phrasing-packs — Regulatory Phrasing Packs
- **Request:** [`requests/regulatory-phrasing-packs.md`](requests/regulatory-phrasing-packs.md)
- **Definitive features:** the phrasing-pack format (qualified-professional reviewer metadata, label, coverage notes — §5.2 items 1–4); the information-not-advice label on every example, download, and notification (§5.2 items 2–3); per-pack coverage notes with disclosed gaps (§5.2 item 4); versioning with reviewer + review date + rules version, and `outdated`/withdrawn lifecycle (§5.2 items 5, 10); static rendering and plain-text/markdown download (§5.4); opt-in, tracking-free changelog/RSS update notifications (§5.2 item 11); free core packs mirrorable from the offline pack (§5.2 item 8); paid maintenance as an optional convenience that never changes content or labelling (§5.2 item 9).
- **Decisions:** D1 — Re-review interval per jurisdiction class; D2 — Sector working groups; D3 — Language/tier policy (English governs, reviewed-only translations, reviewer metadata per pack).
- **Open questions / risks:** digest "Open:" (`re-review interval; sector working groups`) and spec §10 (re-review interval per jurisdiction class; whether sector packs need a working group and how groups form) are both decided (D1–D2). Risk: examples drifting into compliance/certification framing — guarded by the label regime and the independent-assurance boundary.

### experiments-and-metrics — Experiments and Metrics
- **Request:** [`requests/experiments-and-metrics.md`](requests/experiments-and-metrics.md)
- **Definitive features:** a study registration template + public registry (question, hypothesis, method, data sources, population, duration, success criteria — §5.2 item 1); a consent/retention model (§5.2 item 3); the ordered method preference (voluntary records → consented surveys → public artifacts → bounded observation; fingerprinting/cross-site tracking prohibited — §5.2 item 2); the willingness-to-pay safeguard (never gates a free-floor feature — §5.2 item 4); the multilingual-equivalence method (meaning, not wording, using translation statuses — §5.2 item 5); review-behaviour measurement without AI-use inference (§5.2 item 6); equal-prominence publication of negative/null findings (§5.2 item 7); versioned corrections (§5.2 item 8); open study artifacts (§5.2 item 11); no rankings/scores of participants (RL-9 — §5.2 item 9).
- **Decisions:** D1 — Review body for study registrations; D2 — Third-party recruiters for survey panels.
- **Open questions / risks:** digest "Open:" (`review body; third-party recruiters`) and spec §10 (review body jointly with governance; whether survey panels may use third-party recruiters and under what privacy terms) are both decided (D1–D2). Risk: experiments sliding into continuous telemetry — guarded by the privacy-respecting-analytics boundary and the no-fingerprinting red line.

### standards-engagement — Standards Engagement

- **Definitive features:** the engagement policy + mandate template (documents, fora, representatives, forbidden zone — §5.2 item 3); the readiness-precondition record (schema/label/checker stability, independent implementation, published pilots — §5.2 item 1) plus the meaningful-adoption gate reference (cross-cutting); the forum-selection criteria (neutrality, openness, governance quality, IP terms, cost, exit — §5.2 item 2); IP-terms review with rejection of third-party-assignable terms (§5.2 item 4); funding safeguards with disclosure (§5.2 item 5); institutional-capture limits + exit safeguards (§5.2 items 6–7); public progress reporting in the changelog (§5.2 item 8).

### independent-assurance-and-certification — Independent Assurance and Certification
- **Request:** [`requests/independent-assurance-and-certification.md`](requests/independent-assurance-and-certification.md)

- **Open questions / risks:** digest "Open:" (`adoption metric; whether it ever exists`) and spec §10 (the adoption metric; whether the programme exists at all — a governed decision, not an assumption) are both decided (D1–D2). Risk: assurance becoming a paywall that redefines the free mark as inferior (RL-1/RL-4) — guarded by the separation and baseline-untouched rules.
