# The +AI Declaration

AI is already in the room.

It helps us write, think, analyse, design, code, research, calculate, create and decide. Pretending otherwise is absurd.

But the bigger question is not whether AI was used. It is who takes responsibility for the result.

AI can generate answers, code, reports, images, arguments and designs. It cannot take responsibility for sending, deploying or publishing them.

A human can.

That is what +AI means:

AI helped. I take responsibility.

When I place +AI after my name, I am saying AI materially assisted the work. I am not hiding that fact, and I am not blaming the machine if something goes wrong.

I chose to use AI. I chose what to accept, reject and release. I take responsibility for that choice.

Eric Mourant +AI

+AI is not an apology, warning label, certificate, or claim that AI created everything. It is a declaration of ownership.

A signature has long meant: I stand behind this.

In the age of AI, we need one addition: AI helped me get here.

The boundary between “human-made” and “AI-made” is breaking down. Work moves between people, models, software and data. The better question is:

Who is willing to put their name on the result?

+AI draws that line.

Use AI openly. Use it creatively. But when you publish the result, do not hide behind the machine.

Put your name on it. Take responsibility. If AI materially helped, add +AI.

No permission, registration, membership, certification or gatekeeper.

Use it wherever human responsibility should remain visible in AI-assisted work.

The notation is small. The principle is not.

Use AI openly.
Keep responsibility human.

By using +AI, I acknowledge that AI materially assisted this work. I chose to accept, publish, transmit, deploy or otherwise release the result, and I take responsibility for that decision and for the work as presented.

AI helped. I take responsibility.

+AI

by Eric Mourant +AI
