+AI Post Nominal Description

+AI

AI helped.
I take responsibility.

The person or organisation whose name carried this link is making a simple declaration: artificial intelligence materially assisted the work, and they accept responsibility for releasing it.

THE WARM-UP

What the mark says

01
AI participated
AI materially helped create, analyse, transform or present the work.
02
A human chose
A person or organisation knowingly adopted and released the result.
03
Responsibility stays human
The named party does not transfer accountability to an AI system or provider.
THE TRAINING PLAN

Transparent assistance.
Visible accountability.

The mark is an accountability declaration, not a claim that AI is the author and not a guarantee that the work is correct.

It does not say how much of the work came from AI. It says AI materially influenced the result and an identifiable person or organisation stands behind the decision to use it.

Three characters make both participation and responsibility visible.
